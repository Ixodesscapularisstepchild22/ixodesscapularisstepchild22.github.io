🔍 What is lsposed-location-replay?

lsposed-location-replay is a powerful LSPosed module designed for Android devices. It helps developers and researchers test how apps behave in different real-world locations. The module records the complete positioning environment of a physical place—including GPS fixes, WiFi scans, CellInfo data, NMEA sentences, and GnssStatus—and then replays that exact environment to selected apps.

Think of it as a "time machine" for your location services. You visit a place, record everything about its positioning signals, and later replay that environment to test how your apps respond—all on devices you own and control.

Important: This tool is intended for legitimate testing and research purposes only on devices you own. Always respect app terms of service and applicable laws.

✨ Key Features

  • 🛰️ Complete GPS Recording: Capture precise GPS fixes including latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, and accuracy.
  • 📡 WiFi Scan Replay: Record nearby WiFi access points and their signal strengths, then replay them as if you were physically present.
  • 📶 CellInfo Replay: Capture cell tower information including MCC, MNC, LAC, CID, and signal strength.
  • 🔤 NMEA Support: Record raw NMEA sentences for the most precise location data replay.
  • 🧭 GnssStatus Replay: Simulate satellite status, including satellite IDs, signal-to-noise ratios, and elevation/azimuth angles.
  • 🎯 Scoped App Control: Choose exactly which apps receive the replayed location data. Other apps continue using real location.
  • 📁 Easy Record/Replay: Simple interface for recording environments and saving them as files for later replay.

⚙️ How It Works

Recording Phase

1. Open the lsposed-location-replay app on your Android device.

2. Choose "Record New Environment" and give it a name (e.g., "Downtown Office").

3. Walk around the target location for a few minutes. The app will capture all positioning data—GPS satellites, WiFi networks, cell towers, and more.

4. Stop recording and save. The environment file is now stored on your device.

Replay Phase

1. Open the app and select "Replay Environment."

2. Choose the saved environment you want to replay.

3. Select which apps should receive the replayed location (scoped apps).

4. Start the replay. The selected apps will now see the location data from your recording, as if your device were physically at that location.

What Gets Replayed

The module replays a complete positioning "picture" including:

  • 📍 GPS fixes: Lat/long, altitude, speed, bearing, accuracy
  • 📶 WiFi scans: SSID, BSSID, RSSI, frequency
  • 📡 CellInfo: Tower IDs, signal strength, network type
  • 🔤 NMEA sentences: Raw NMEA 0183 data (GGA, RMC, GSA, etc.)
  • 🛰️ GnssStatus: Satellite constellation info, SNRs, elevation/azimuth
