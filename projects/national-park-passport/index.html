National Park Passport

This personal Tableau project helps me track National Park Passport stamps and plan trips to collect the ones I am missing. It separates official stamp groups from additional stamps and organizes locations by state and region across the lower 48 states.

PROJECT IMAGENational Park Passport Tableau dashboardReplace with an approved screenshot or original visual

How the dashboard works

The overview shows region and state progress, with counts of unique stamps collected. A state detail view combines a map and a table of official stamp opportunities. Filters distinguish visited official and extra stamps, while dashboard actions help move from a regional view to specific places.

Data choices

The data separates stamp-level records from distinct locations so counts and maps answer different questions correctly. Retired locations can be filtered out for planning. The visualization is a personal utility as well as an example of Tableau dashboard design and data modeling.
