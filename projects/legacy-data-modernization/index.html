Legacy data modernization

Modernizing a large legacy data estate starts with understanding its contents. In a public-sector consulting program, source systems included relational tables and mainframe files with long histories and sensitive data. The work required careful sequencing so teams could identify fields and business rules before transformation.

Quality workflow

The approach covered profiling, metadata and lineage, cleansing, classification, matching and merging, standardization, transformation and validation. Quality monitoring and issue resolution provided a way to address exceptions after the initial load.

Practical emphasis

Definitions, source-to-target rules and repeatable checks matter as much as moving bytes. I contributed BI, data quality and documentation skills within a larger modernization effort. Specific system details and sensitive records are omitted.
